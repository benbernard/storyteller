# Storyteller identity

Storyteller is the project name and the web/Android app name. The chosen Bookwave
mark is an open book becoming sound waves, in emerald and mint. It keeps the
existing warm reading palette and appears on a pale green tile in both themes.
The Android application ID and pairing/download storage identities are unchanged.

The canonical transparent PNG is
[`storyteller-mark.png`](../tools/audiobooks/listener-web/storyteller-mark.png).
The Android drawable is the same file. The browser header and app headings expose
the text name to accessibility services and treat the adjacent mark as decorative.
The adaptive launcher uses the same mark with an inset for system masks.

The mark was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, transparent
background enabled. The owner selected Bookwave from six concepts. A prompt for reproducing its visual direction is:

> Design a polished standalone app icon for Storyteller, a personal audiobook
> creation and listening system. One open book whose pages transform into three
> clean sound-wave arcs on the right. Communicate books plus audio.
> Deep emerald and pale mint, a small warm cream accent, strong recognizable
> silhouette, restrained dimensional paper layering, elegant and friendly.
> No letters, words, border, headphones, or surrounding scene. Center the symbol
> with generous safe space on a transparent background. Make it readable at small
> launcher sizes and suitable for a warm cream or dark reading interface.

The source is a 1280 × 1280 PNG. Preserve the transparent original when deriving
platform assets. Do not stretch it or add text inside the icon.
